My Journey

From process engineering to enterprise software.

Dorababu began in chemical and process engineering, working across pharma and oil and gas environments where small deviations in temperature, pressure, flow, or yield demanded disciplined root-cause analysis.

That operating mindset now shapes his software work: he approaches production bugs, slow execution plans, legacy code, and deployment problems as systems to be traced, measured, and improved.

His path moved from Chemical Engineering to Process Engineer, then into Software Development as a Project Trainee, Software Developer, and Senior Software Developer focused on DashMagiq, enterprise DLP integrations, SQL Server performance, backend APIs, and Azure DevOps automation.
